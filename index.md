# VibeDrive

Real-time stem-driven music for the road. VibeDrive separates your tracks into stems on our cloud backend and plays them back with effects driven by how you're driving.

VibeDrive is a mobile app for Android, distributed exclusively on the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.drivex.vibedrive).
